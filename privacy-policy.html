Padosi Multiplayer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Padosi Multiplayer ("the game"), published by Zaan Infotech ("we"), handles. We built the game to collect as little as possible.

Information you enter

You can type a player name. It is stored only on your device and is shown to other players in the same match. We do not ask for your email, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or location.

Online and local play

Online rooms use Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay), provided by Unity Technologies. To connect you to other players, these services create an anonymous player ID and process your device's IP address. This data is used only to run multiplayer matches. Unity's privacy policy is at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Local Wi-Fi play finds rooms on your own network and does not send data to us.

Crash reports and diagnostics

The game may send anonymous crash reports and basic performance diagnostics (device model, operating system version, error logs) through Unity, so we can fix bugs. These reports are not used to identify you.

Advertising

The game is free and shows a small number of full-screen ads between matches (never during play). Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ads privacy in the game. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers named above (Unity for multiplayer and diagnostics, Google AdMob for ads) so the game can work and stay free.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

No account is created. Uninstalling the game removes your player name and settings from your device. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
